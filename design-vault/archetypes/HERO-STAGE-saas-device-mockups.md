## Signature Interactive Pattern: The Annotated Product Tour Stage (MOCK-30)

### Spatial Geometry & Interactive Architecture:
* **Stage Canvas:** Centered 1120px container with soft aurora ambient backlight.
* **Central Showcase (Browser Viewport):**
  - Dimensions: 16:10 aspect ratio (`w-full max-w-4xl mx-auto rounded-2xl border border-white/20 shadow-2xl bg-card`).
  - Video Play Trigger: Centered circular action button (`w-16 h-16 rounded-full bg-primary/90 text-primary-foreground shadow-lg flex items-center justify-center hover:scale-105 transition-transform`).
* **Floating Interactive Pointer Badges:**
  - Badge A (Top-Left): Frosted pill tag (`bg-background/80 backdrop-blur-md border border-border px-3 py-1.5 rounded-full text-xs font-semibold shadow-md`) with connecting hairline vector indicator to a specific UI feature.
  - Badge B (Right-Center): Interactive tooltip showing *"Active Telemetry: Live Analytics Stream"*.
  - Badge C (Bottom-Left): Verification chip *"End-to-End Encrypted"*.
* **Mobile Invariant (375px):**
  - Floating badges cannot overlap or clutter touch viewports.
  - On screens `<= 768px`, the floating badges detach from the visual frame and render as an **interactive tabbed feature list or horizontal swipeable chip row** directly beneath the video preview!
