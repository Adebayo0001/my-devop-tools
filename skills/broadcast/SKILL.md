# Workflow: broadcast (Evergreen Proof-of-Work & Client Acquisition Engine)

## Dependencies
**MANDATORY:** This skill depends entirely on the `adebayo-authority-engine` skill. When invoked, the agent MUST read `adebayo-authority-engine` to enforce positioning, the 4-post daily batch rule, and the natural human-voice structure.

Invoke with:
- `/broadcast` — Reads the current workspace/terminal to see what was built today, and generates the 4 daily posts based on actual engineering lessons.
- `/broadcast intel` — Generates 4 daily posts breaking down Industry AI Intelligence (frontier models, architecture).
- `/broadcast off-day` — Generates 4 daily posts pulling evergreen engineering lessons (from `ai-dev-standards` or `devsecops_ai_security_roadmap.md`).

---

## 1. The Client Confidentiality & Privacy Gate (MANDATORY STEP 1)
**NEVER assume permission to share project names, client identities, or proprietary business logic.**
- Default to **Anonymized Technical Positioning** (e.g. *"a multi-window broadcast application"*, *"an offline-first AI desktop system"*).
- Focus on the *engineering mechanism* (state sync, zero-trust IPC, design tokens, CI gates) rather than confidential client domain secrets.

---

## 2. Core Positioning & The "Vibe Coder vs. Architect" Framework
Every post must subtly or overtly communicate the core differentiator:
* **The Average Vibe Coder**: Types loose prompts, generates spaghetti code, has no security or tests, crashes on Day 3.
* **The AI Systems Architect (You)**: Applies senior engineering discipline (DevSecOps, strict TypeScript, zero-trust) to ship rock-solid software in 14 days.

---

## 3. High-Intent Algorithm Keywords (Auto-Injected)
Every post must naturally integrate high-search-volume keywords:
- Primary: `#AIEngineering #SoftwareArchitecture #MVPDevelopment #FullStack #CustomSoftware`
- Discipline: `#EnterpriseSoftware #ZeroTrust #CodeQuality #TechnicalDebt #DevOps`

---

## 4. Output Protocol (The Pipeline)
1. Generate the 4 posts as a daily batch following the `adebayo-authority-engine` format. Do not use desperate CTAs.
2. Save each draft as a `.md` file directly into `C:\Users\user\Desktop\Lifestream\content\queue\`.
3. Inform the user that the Telegram bot (`@my_build_publisher_bot`) will automatically pick these up.
